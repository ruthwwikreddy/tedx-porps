03 — Overview
03 / 20
About The Platform

A student-led crucible for transformative local ideas, global mindsets, and unfiltered dialogue.

TEDxPORPS Youth is built on the belief that young minds possess profound clarity regarding the structures governing our world. Operating under official license from TED, our event brings together dynamic speakers and an engaged community to challenge conventional wisdom.

This is an independently organized event operated under the leadership of student curators and faculty advisors at P. Obul Reddy Public School.
